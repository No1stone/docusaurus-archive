# Workflow: Refactor

> **개요:** 리팩터 워크플로 순서.

Control plane: [`../orchestration.md`](../orchestration.md)

```text
Master: Intent & constraints
    ↓
Master: Context Resolution (Architecture / Patterns / Templates)
    ↓
Implementer (behavior-preserving)
    ↓
Reviewer (behavior-preserving focus)
    ↓
Tester
    ↓
QA (scope & constraints)
    ↓
Master finalize
```
